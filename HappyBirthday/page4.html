A Letter to You

💌
My Dearest,

There are words I've been meaning to say — not just today, but on every ordinary Tuesday, every quiet evening, every moment you laughed and didn't realize how much light you were giving the room.

You are one of those rare people who make the world softer just by being in it. Your kindness, your laugh, your way of caring deeply — these are not small things. They are everything.

I am so endlessly grateful the universe put us in each other's orbit. Today, on your birthday, I want you to feel every bit of that love reflected back at you.

May this year bring you all the things your heart quietly wishes for. May it be full of colour, warmth, surprise, and joy. May you always know you are deeply, completely, unconditionally loved.

With all the love I have, Always yours 💕
